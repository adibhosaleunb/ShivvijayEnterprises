Pandemic Support

During the dreadful situation due to the Covid Pandemic, Shivvijay Enterprises has been working relentlessly to aid our clients involved in processing of health care and medicinal products. The company has been working for Garware Bestretch Ltd, manufacturer of elastomers necessary for essential health care products like face masks and hospital rubber sheets. Shivvijay Enterprises has been manufacturing equipment required for production of essential pharmaceutical capsule processing machineries for Utopia Optovision Limited.

Our team has been working dedicatedly in the time of this pandemic with a will to help fellow people as much as possible in these crucial times.
