About

About Us

Over the past two decades, Shivvijay Enterprises has delivered innovative, customized and competitive products and solutions for processing and storage in pharmaceutical, dairy, polymer, chemical and material processing industries. We have been enabling our customers to grow production capacities way ahead of their peers. Shivvijay Enterprises covers wide range of products from the manufacturing of Special Purpose Machines to the production and implementation of pressing, conveying, mixing and storing equipment.

The company specializes in manufacturing of Special Purpose Machines for heating, cooling, pressing and extruding. As a processing and storage equipment provider, the company has designed and manufactured numerous solutions from Drums, Pallets, hydraulic presses to Horizontal and Vertical Multipurpose Tanks. With a considerable experience in material processing, the company has assured economic and purpose-oriented solutions for manufacturing products in Stainless Steel, Aluminium, Mild Steel or synthetic polymer materials.

Shivvijay Enterprises has designed and implemented over 9 variants in high volume storage and processing tanks up to 50,000 litres. The company has manufactured products using smart automation in dairy processing and CIP systems. It has set its overall manufacturing standards as required by the Defence establishments.

Our aim stands clear on :

  • Meeting discussed timelines.
  • Offering our customers with best possible price.
