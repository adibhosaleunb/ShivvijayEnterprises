From Our CEO

From Our CEO

Vijaysinh D. Mane - CEO

It has been a great pleasure working besides our team at Shivvijay Enterprises for 19 years. Our team has manufactured a wide range of products making certain to behold the satisfaction of the customers while providing well engineered solution to their requirements. I am thankful towards my team for making it to all what we have achieved till now. I am thankful to all our customers for their confidence and trust in us.

We have been providing engineered solutions for manufacture of Special Purpose machines in food, chemical, polymer, pharmaceutical and material processing industries. We design and develop for applications demanding processes of heating, chilling, pressing, extruding and conveying. We have been manufacturing pressurized tanks, conveyors, hydraulic system, pallets, drums and several other storage and processing equipment.

We at Shivvijay Enterprises have always focused on “first time right, everything right” approach on the quality of our products. It is of utmost importance to us to provide durability and quality in all the products we manufacture. We ensure consideration of our customers satisfaction in terms of time, quality and pricing of our products. We have always valued and maintained transparent and lucid relationships with our customers and suppliers.

We work as a strong and reliable team for design, development and production of our products. Our team implements the ISO 9001:2015 standards across the lifecycle of all our products. We have always ensured prompt customer support to our customers prior to sales as well as after the completion of sales.

We have achieved a business growth of 4 times since the last two years (2019-2021). We are visionary on growing our organization and are consistently venturing into new opportunities in Defence projects and Innovative Design and manufacture of customized products. I appreciate and look forward to the continued initiative and efforts of our team.
