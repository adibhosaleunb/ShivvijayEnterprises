    Welcome to Shivvijay Enterprises

    We design and manufacture according to your needs.

    Design and Manufacture of Engineering Products

    We specialize in designing engineering solutions, special purpose machinery, conveyors, Plain rollers, knurled rollers, metal art furniture, decor, pressurized tanks for Chemical, Pharmaceutical and Milk storage, CIP systems and other customized storage and processing equipment.

    Quality Assurance

    We have built up a strong PLAN-DO-CHECK-ACT cycle within our management and technical team to ensure quality of our products. We have always considered quality as the backbone of our organization and always certain abiding by it.

    Over the past two decades, Shivvijay Enterprises has delivered innovative, customized and competitive products and solutions for processing and storage in pharmaceutical, dairy, polymer, chemical and material processing industries. We have been enabling our customers to grow production capacities way ahead of their peers. Shivvijay Enterprises covers wide range of products from the manufacturing of Special Purpose Machines to the production and implementation of pressing, conveying, mixing and storing equipment.

    The company specializes in manufacturing of Special Purpose Machines for conveying, cutting, heating, cooling, pressing and extruding. As a processing and storage equipment provider, the company has designed and manufactured numerous solutions from Drums, Pallets, leaf truck trolleys to Horizontal and Vertical Multipurpose Tanks. With a considerable experience in material processing, the company has assured economic and purpose-oriented solutions for manufacturing products in Stainless Steel, Aluminium, Mild Steel and synthetic polymer materials.
